# Arkade Heroes — Terms of Use

**Last updated:** 2026-07-29

By creating a wallet or playing Arkade Heroes, you accept these terms. If you do not
accept them, do not play.

These terms are written to be read. They are short, and they say what actually
happens rather than hiding it in defined terms.

---

## 1. The five things that matter

Read this section even if you read nothing else.

1. **You are playing with real Bitcoin.** In-game "sats" are satoshis — real bitcoin
   on the Arkade network. They are not points, credits, or tokens. Spending them
   spends real money.

2. **We never hold your money or your heroes.** Your wallet is created in your
   browser and its private keys never leave your device. We cannot move your funds.
   We cannot freeze them. We cannot take them.

3. **If you lose your recovery phrase, everything is gone permanently.** There is no
   password reset, no support ticket, no administrator who can help. We do not have
   your keys, so there is nothing for us to restore. Write your recovery phrase down
   and keep it somewhere safe.

4. **Some game modes destroy heroes forever.** A death match burns the loser. Fusion
   burns the heroes you combine. Burned means destroyed on-chain — the asset stops
   existing, and nobody can bring it back.

5. **You can lose everything you stake.** Only risk what you can afford to lose
   completely.

---

## 2. What this is

Arkade Heroes is a game about breeding, battling and trading collectable heroes.
Heroes and items are assets on Arkade, a Bitcoin layer-2 network. Ownership lives on
that network, not on our servers.

Your heroes are yours in a stronger sense than in an ordinary online game — we cannot
confiscate them, and your account cannot be "banned" out of existence. The same design
means we cannot give anything back either. Both halves follow from the same fact.

## 3. Your wallet is your responsibility

- Keys are generated in your browser and stay there. We never see them.
- **We cannot recover your wallet** — not with proof of purchase, not with proof of
  identity, not under any circumstances.
- Anyone with your recovery phrase controls everything you own. Never share it. **We
  will never ask for it.** Anyone who does is trying to rob you.
- Clearing your browser storage without having saved your recovery phrase destroys
  your access permanently.
- Keeping your device secure is up to you.

## 4. Transactions are final

Once an action is confirmed on the network, it cannot be undone — by you, by us, or by
anyone. There is no chargeback and no undo.

This includes actions you did not mean to take: a hero sold at the wrong price, sats
sent to the wrong address, a death match entered by mistake. We cannot reverse a
transaction, unwind a trade, restore a burned hero, or refund a fee.

**Check before you confirm. There is no second chance.**

## 5. Fees, and what you actually pay

Some actions cost satoshis: breeding, renaming a hero, death-match entry, tournament
buy-ins, and a fee on marketplace sales. Current amounts are shown before you confirm.

**Marketplace sales.** Listing costs nothing. If your item sells, the sale is split
automatically by the contract: you receive the ask minus the marketplace fee, and the
fee goes to the game treasury. **If it never sells, you are never charged** — you can
take it back at any time and pay nothing.

**Network fees** charged by Arkade are separate from ours and outside our control.

**Fees can change.** Changes apply to new actions only. An offer already resting on
the market keeps the terms it was created with, because those terms are fixed in its
on-chain contract and cannot be edited afterwards — not even by us.

## 6. Permanent loss

These are not figures of speech.

- **Death match** — the losing hero is destroyed on-chain. In absorb mode both heroes
  are destroyed and a new one is created for the winner. Staked sats go to the winner.
- **Fusion** — the heroes you combine are destroyed to create the new one.
- **Wagered matches and tournaments** — your stake or buy-in goes to the winners.
- **Breeding** — the fee is spent whatever the result. Offspring are determined by the
  game's genetics and randomness. There is no guaranteed outcome and no refund for one
  you dislike.

**Do not enter these modes with a hero you are not prepared to lose.**

## 7. Fairness

Match, tournament, gauntlet and trial results are designed to be checkable by you.
The game publishes the inputs and a commitment beforehand, and your client can
recompute the result and confirm it matches. Where a verify button is offered, use it.

Verification proves the result follows from the published inputs. It does not prove
the game is free of bugs.
